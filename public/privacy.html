Privacy notice

Draft for legal review. Last updated 2 October 2026.

What we collect

When you request an AI Visibility Snapshot, we collect your business name, website, location, your name and your email address. We also record how you found us (for example, campaign tags in the link you clicked and the referring website).

Why we collect it

We use this information to prepare your Snapshot, to email it to you, and to follow up with you about it. We do not sell your personal data.

Where it is stored

Your request is stored with our service providers: GitHub (website hosting) and our own server (request records). These providers may store data outside Singapore.

Our website loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when you visit.

How long we keep it

We keep your request for as long as needed to provide the Snapshot and any follow-up services, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Your choices

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act you can ask to access or correct your personal data, or withdraw consent for us to use it. Email hello@answeready.ai.
